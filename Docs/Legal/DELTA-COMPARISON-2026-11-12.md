# Delta comparison —12 November2026

Delta's current official repository declares AGPLv3; its US App Store listing is live. The exact checked source revision and license/README hashes are recorded in the companionJSON. No App Store-specific permission is inferred from unrelated forks or older README wording.

This is meaningful evidence that an emulator using copyleft-licensed source can be offered in the Store, and that the license-family label alone is not an automatic Apple rejection. EmuDen's intended model is broadly similar: a licensed emulator with user-supplied games, source/notices, original presentation and no commercial ROM download service. Apple4.7 explicitly permits the emulator category.

It does not establish the legal terms/permissions/agreements under which every Delta component is distributed, or transfer publisher rights, brand clearance, or upstream permissions to EmuDen. Delta supports a different set of systems and dependencies; this3DS core has its own content-crypto/system-resource scope. AGPL and GPL share relevant copyleft concepts, but are not interchangeable license names.

Practical assessment: the repaired3DS-only candidate has a credible path to Apple review, with concrete dependency/resource-source issues now addressed. It should not be described as legally impossible just because of GPL. The current project hold remains while the actual Apple/GPL arrangement and relevant rights questions are resolved; there is no guarantee of Apple acceptance or legal clearance.
