## Clarification following the Delta comparison

The absence of documented publisher permission is not a finding that all cover display is unlawful, and direct publisher permission is not the only possible legal basis. Applicable licenses, service terms and context-specific copyright exceptions must be considered. The earlier permission-or-removal wording was too categorical. Delta's own README describes automatic cover art for imported games; its source credits OpenVGDB. Its code is published under AGPLv3 and it has a current App Store listing. Those facts support the feasibility of an artwork-enabled open-source emulator, but do not establish Delta's particular publisher arrangements or transfer rights to EmuDen.

Identifying a user's imported game with a cover thumbnail and operating a public collection of newly generated character illustrations present different factual uses. This is a reason to assess the uses separately, not a ruling that one is always permitted and the other always prohibited. U.S. fair use is case-specific. Apple approval is also distinct from a court's copyright determination.

The open-source license is a compliance obligation, not evidence that a GPL-family app is automatically barred from the App Store. Apple's standard EULA expressly recognizes certain open-source licensing permissions. We still need to document the applicable license obligations and actual distribution arrangement; this review does not require replacing the core or obtaining a new license merely because Delta uses a different GPL-family license.

Additional primary sources: [Delta README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rileytestut/Delta/main/README.md), [Delta's artwork-provider credit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rileytestut/Delta/main/Delta/Base.lproj/Settings.storyboard), [current App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688), [Apple standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/), and [U.S. Copyright Office fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/).
