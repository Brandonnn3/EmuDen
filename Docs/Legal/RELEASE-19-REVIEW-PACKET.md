## GPL and the Apple distribution route

The original app/bridge/widget/tool code is GPL-3.0-or-later under the owner's recorded authorization. The pinned Azahar core is GPL-3.0-or-later, MoltenVK Apache-2.0, and bundled Homebrew Menu retains its attribution terms. Complete notices, modified sources/patches, pinned upstream source and rebuilding instructions are supplied. Builds 17–18 have public matching source downloads with anonymous checksum evidence. Build 19 must publish its own exact matching package before distribution.

GPLv3 sections 5–6 require applicable license/source/build material; section 10 addresses further restrictions. Source publication satisfies a concrete source-access need but does not settle every distribution condition. Apple's Standard EULA includes an open-source exception in its copying/reverse-engineering clause; it should not be described as a blanket waiver of every redistribution or platform-use condition. Apple's Developer Program agreement requires applicable FOSS compliance and contains minimum EULA/distribution terms. No tailored EULA has been filed.

The specific review questions are whether the actual chosen Apple terms and any copy protection add prohibited restrictions for this combined program, whether any GPLv3 Installation Information duty applies to this transaction, and what precise EULA/distribution arrangement satisfies both sets of obligations. Rebuilding with one's own signing is documented; no owner signing secret is published. A GPL-family app's presence on the Store demonstrates feasibility, not a transferable legal determination. Do not declare a blanket GPL ban or pretend these questions were resolved by the owner's permission.
