EmuDen brings compatible Nintendo 3DS, PlayStation and GameCube software into a native iPhone and iPad library.

• Import your own compatible game and homebrew files through Files.
• Play using touchscreen controls or a compatible game controller.
• Use dual-screen 3DS layouts or single-screen PlayStation/GameCube play.
• Choose 3DS picture sizing, 1×–3× resolution and per-game graphics preferences.
• Keep native in-game saves, PlayStation/GameCube memory cards, Quick Save/Load, dated history and automatic resume points.
• Open recent games with local Continue Playing and Recent Games widgets.
• Select your own cover, background and profile images, or connect a compatible artwork catalog of your choice to fill matching 3DS games.

PlayStation imports support CHD v5, CUE with all BIN tracks, and PS-X EXE homebrew. GameCube imports accept MEM1 DOL and complete uncompressed GameCube ISO/GCM or Dolphin-format GameCube CISO; RVZ and Wii discs are unsupported. 3DS imports require compatible decrypted files. Imported software requires adult access confirmation. Compatibility and performance vary by title and device.

No commercial game, proprietary console firmware, BIOS, Mii head models or console keys are included or offered for download. Only import files you have the right to use. The app is free; optional Apple tips, where available, do not unlock features.
