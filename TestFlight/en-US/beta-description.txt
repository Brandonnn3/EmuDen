Welcome to EmuDen, your pocket console collection.

This internal beta introduces a native iPhone and iPad game library with Nintendo 3DS emulation, dual-screen layouts, touchscreen controls, compatible game controllers, audio, persistent in-game saves, quick save/load, dated snapshot history and automatic resume points. iPhone Duo is the primary design and performance target.

Per-game graphics profiles offer 1×–3× resolution, texture sampling, optional automatic quality, known-shader preparation and frame timing. Save history retains ten manual and three automatic points per game, including optional five-minute active-play checkpoints. A cinematic library downloads original background illustrations and caches them offline. Continue Playing and Recent Games widgets use local thumbnails, and a profile-picture chooser keeps your portrait separate from Mii resource status.

Add your own compatible, decrypted game files using the Files picker, or launch the included open-source 3DS Homebrew Menu to test the emulator without importing a commercial game. Games and Nintendo console system resources are not included.

This version supports 3DS only. Other console cores are planned for later development and are not available in this build.

Performance and compatibility vary by game. CPU interpretation is used; JIT is disabled. An original generic 3D guest head replaces missing Mii resources, but original Mii faces require console-provided system data. Castle Club and complete Mii compatibility remain under test.

No app account, subscription or purchase is required. Use TestFlight feedback to report the game, device, scene, FPS and steps to reproduce a problem. Please do not attach game files, firmware, console keys or personal Mii databases.
